About

Mammootty is an Indian actor and producer who has worked mainly in Malayalam films for more than five decades.

His original name is Muhammad Kutty Panaparambil Ismail. He was born on 7 September 1951 in Chandiroor, Kerala. Before becoming an actor, he studied law and worked as a lawyer.

He has acted in more than 400 films in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi and English. He is also known for supporting charity and healthcare projects in Kerala.
